# 1. Research: what's hard about getting around Japan

Researched September 2026. The trip ends before October 23, 2026.

## Main pain points, ranked for us

| # | Problem | Why it matters | What the app can do |
|---|---------|----------------|---------------------|
| 1 | **Language** | Big cities have English signs and menus. Small family restaurants, izakayas and rural spots often don't. Staff are rarely confident in English. | Show-cards with big Japanese text to hand to staff, phrases with Hebrew/English pronunciation and spoken audio, a decoder for common signs, shortcuts to camera translation |
| 2 | **Cash** | Shrines, small izakayas, older ryokans, coin lockers and some taxis are cash only. Guides suggest carrying ¥10,000–20,000. | A cash guide: which ATMs take foreign cards (7-Eleven, Japan Post), a "do I have enough cash?" check, coin and banknote reference |
| 3 | **Prices in a foreign currency** | Prices in ¥ are hard to picture quickly (¥1,000 is not obviously "≈ ₪X"). | A fast offline converter ¥ ↔ ₪ ↔ $, plus mental-math anchors ("¥1,000 ≈ ₪__") |
| 4 | **Trains and IC cards** | Welcome Suica Mobile is iPhone-only (iOS 17.2+), valid 180 days, no deposit. In Hokkaido it only works in some areas, and you can't ride continuously across regions: exit the gates and re-enter. | A short Suica guide, how to top up (cash at station machines), what to do when the gate beeps |
| 5 | **Etiquette** | Onsen rules (tattoos, washing first), no tipping, shoes off, quiet on trains, rubbish bins are rare, eating while walking is frowned on. | A short do/don't list, readable in two minutes |
| 6 | **Crowds** | A record 42.7M visitors in 2025. Popular spots are packed. | Tip: go at 6–8 AM or after 5 PM. Out of scope for now. |
| 7 | **Emergencies** | Earthquakes, getting sick, lost passport. | Emergency numbers, the Israeli embassy, link to the "Safety tips" disaster-alert app, a "take me to a hospital" card in Japanese |

## Tax-free shopping (relevant to us: the current system)

- **Until October 31, 2026** (our whole trip): show your passport at a
  tax-free store and the 10% consumption tax comes off at the till.
  Generally **¥5,000+ (before tax) per store, same day**. Consumables
  (food, cosmetics) get sealed and can't be opened in Japan.
- **From November 1, 2026:** you pay full price and get a refund at the
  airport, within 90 days. **Not relevant to us**, but worth a line in the
  app for anyone who uses it later.

staff that work offline.
